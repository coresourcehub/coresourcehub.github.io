A search-result thumbnail, news publication, or government website does not alone authorize reuse.

## Adding future images safely

1. Verify the facility identity and whether the photo represents a current or historical site.
2. Record the creator, date if known, canonical original URL, and explicit license or written permission.
3. Confirm commercial/noncommercial restrictions and modification/share-alike requirements before placing the image.
4. Use a practical-size image, lazy loading, descriptive alt text, and caption with source/license links.
5. Keep the placeholder until attribution and provenance are clear; do not hotlink news sites without authorization.
6. Check image loading on mobile after each deployment. A Wikimedia-hosted image requires an internet connection and may fail if the provider's URL or policy changes.
