About CO Resource Hub

CO Resource Hub is an independent project organizing publicly available Colorado corrections information and developing accessible research resources. The searchable policy library preserves original public documents while presenting separately labeled summaries and navigation aids.

No account or login is required to browse this website. Sensitive family reports will not be stored in this public GitHub repository.

Publication status: Policy Library research beta available; source-fidelity review ongoing. Family experience questionnaire is hosted independently on CryptPad.
